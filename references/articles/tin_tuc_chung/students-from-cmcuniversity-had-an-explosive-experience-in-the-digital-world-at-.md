## Students from CMC University had an explosive experience in the digital world at the Vietnam International Innovation Expo 2023 (VIIE 2023)

On October 31st, CMC Uni students had the opportunity to participate in this international exhibition, which took place within the framework of the event at the Hoa Lac High-Tech Park in Hanoi.

The event provided students with the opportunity to witness a glimpse of the future technology landscape through the innovations presented by leading technology companies, both domestic and international. Additionally, it allowed them to update their knowledge about the current state and trends in technology development in Vietnam and around the world. Students from CMC University actively participated in the Vietnam International Innovation Expo 2023 (VIIE 2023).

The Vietnam International Innovation Expo 2023 (VIIE 2023) opened in the morning of October 28, 2023, as part of the inauguration event for the new working facility of the National Innovation Center (NIC) in Hoa Lac High-tech Park, Hanoi.

Of special significance, the National Innovation Center (NIC) is one of the key strategic partners of CMC University. Specifically, during the launch ceremony of CMC University, which is the first digital university model in Vietnam, held on November 17, 2022, in Hanoi, CMC University, CMC Technology Corporation, and the National Innovation Center (NIC) jointly signed a Memorandum of Understanding to promote innovation and digital transformation.

According to the content of the signed agreement, all parties involved will cooperate in researching, proposing, and implementing innovative solutions, digital transformation initiatives, and the development of digital business environments in various regions and across multiple sectors.

VIIE 2023 is the largest event in Vietnam that serves as a nexus for various elements within the innovation and creative ecosystem. At this event, students from CMC University had the unique opportunity to directly witness the “canvas” of future technology presented by leading domestic and international technology innovators.

From the exhibition booths showcasing and promoting advanced technological products and solutions, the focus was on 8 key areas of NIC: smart factories, smart cities, cybersecurity, digital content, semiconductor industry, hydrogen technology, healthcare technology, and environmental technology. Additionally, there were interactive technology experience zones where products and technology applications were flexibly and dynamically demonstrated.

At VIIE 2023, students from CMC University experienced the pioneering technological product exhibition booth by CMC Technology Corporation (CMC). CMC used a combination of physical product displays and interactive technical presentations with motion graphics to provide visitors with new, visually engaging experiences. Moreover, effective lighting technology applications engaged the senses of participants.

CMC’s exhibition booth attracted significant attention from both domestic and international visitors at VIIE 2023. Among the various technology booths, students from CMC University took pride in taking commemorative photos at CMC Technology Corporation’s exhibition booth.

Furthermore, the technology gateway welcomed students from CMC University to explore and experience various technological highlights, including technology, lighting, colors, and a Holobox with a Metahuman at the end of the tunnel, interacting with and greeting the visitors.

VIIE 2023 not only guided students from CMC University to explore technology experiential spaces and unlock the features and benefits of technology in real-world environments but also opened up a treasure trove of knowledge on innovative and creative technology. Through specialized seminars, students were updated on the current status and development trends of technology in Vietnam and globally.

As part of the activities on October 31st, students from CMC University had the opportunity to participate in the Vietnam GameMaker Conference, a national forum on the Vietnamese gaming industry. The theme of the conference was “Exploring Potential: Taking Vietnamese Game Development to New Heights,” featuring interesting and informative presentations by industry experts. This international forum was the first of its kind in the Vietnamese gaming industry, attracting the participation of the game development ecosystem to exchange ideas, solve problems, provide knowledge, and inspire each other, collectively shaping the future of the gaming industry.

Speakers at the conference including renowned experts and entrepreneurs in the industry, such as Gaby Hien, Director of Business Development at Google Play, Google Group; Jason Ng, Vice President of Garena Group; Nguyen Dinh Khanh, CEO and Co-Founder of Wolffun Games, Vice Chairman of the Vietnam Game Development Association.

VIIE 2023 represents a significant event in Vietnam’s innovation and creative landscape, delivering substantial value to its partners and participants. It fosters opportunities for cooperation, knowledge sharing, and creates a closely interconnected environment for entities within the innovation and creative ecosystem.

As the journey concludes, students from CMC University, and particularly those from the Faculty of Information Technology & Communication, have gained valuable insights into innovative and creative activities in the digital world. They have found the “key” to unlocking the doors of information technology for themselves, motivating them to build a path towards becoming digital citizens, ready to conquer the world of technology in the future.
