Position Available: Computer Science Lecturer

Job Description:

Developing teaching plans, designing, and preparing content for computer science subjects including:

Group 1: Computer Architecture, Computer Networks and Communication, Operating System Principles, Computer System Engineering, Parallel and Distributed Systems, Digital Systems.

Group 2: Introduction to Computer Science, Principles of Programming Languages, Programming Techniques, Object-Oriented Programming, Data Structures and Algorithms, Programming with C++, C#, Python, Web Programming Fundamentals, Web Technology and Programming, Game Programming, Mobile Application Development.

Group 3: Database Foundations, Information Security, Introduction to Artificial Intelligence, Software Technology, Software Development and Design.

Conducting theoretical and practical teaching in-class or online as per the university’s schedule and regulations.

Participating in professional development activities:

Developing training programs.

Guiding graduation projects.

Mentoring students in scientific research.

Preparing students for ITSS exams.

Exam invigilation and grading.

Conducting scientific research as per the university’s regulations.

Undertaking other tasks assigned by the Department Head and as directed by the Principal.

Requirements:

Knowledge and Experience:

Qualifications: Masters or higher in Computer Science, Information Technology, or equivalent fields. Preference for graduates from advanced countries.

Preference for those with a teaching certificate.

Experience in teaching and managing specialized courses.

Research experience in the field, with a preference for those with international publications.

Pedagogical skills: Teaching using diverse and effective methods and techniques, incorporating information technology.

Ability to read and research materials in English.

Good pedagogical and communication skills.

Passionate, dedicated, and enthusiastic about teaching.

Professional ethics, legal compliance, proactive, careful, honest, and high stress tolerance.

Creative thinking and independent thought.

Competitive salary, negotiable based on capabilities, attractive annual income package as per CMC Corporation’s policies.

Healthy, dynamic, friendly work environment with clear career progression paths.

Access to training courses offered by the University and the Corporation.

Employee benefits as per current legal regulations (social insurance, health insurance, unemployment insurance, etc.).

Annual health insurance package specifically for staff and lecturers.

Holiday and New Year bonuses; annual KPI bonuses.

Participation in extracurricular activities: arts, sports, travel organized by the university and the Corporation.
