## CMC University Announces Supplemental Admission for 234 Seats

Following the announcement of admission scores, CMC University has declared supplemental admissions for 234 seats across six programs, with the deadline being September 25.

Specifically, CMC University has decided to offer supplemental admission for all its programs: Information Technology, Computer Science, Business Administration, Graphic Design, Japanese Language, and Korean Language, utilizing two methods of selection – based on national high school graduation exam scores and academic transcripts.

The admission criteria for these programs are as follows:

| No. | Program | AdmissionCombination | NationalHighSchoolGraduationExamScore | AcademicTranscriptScore |
| 1 | InformationTechnology | A00, A01, D01, D90 | 23.0 | 23.0 |
| 2 | ComputerScience | A00, A01, D01, D90 | 23.0 | 23.0 |
| 3 | ComputerScience | A00, A01, C00, D01 | 22.5 | 22.5 |
| 4 | GraphicDesign | A00, A01, C01, D90 | 22.5 | 22.5 |
| 5 | JapaneseLanguage | A01, C00, D01, D06 | 22.0 | 22.0 |
| 6 | KoreanLanguage | A01, C00, D01, D10 | 22.0 | 22.0 |

Discussing the supplemental admissions, a representative of CMC University stated, “The supplemental admission of 234 seats offers a final opportunity for candidates who have yet to choose their preferred field of study and institution based on their interests and capabilities.” The deadline for supplemental admission registration for candidates is 5 PM on September 25, 2023. This deadline may end earlier if the university fills all available seats.

The university representative also mentioned that candidates applying for supplemental admission still have the opportunity to receive scholarships up to 100%. In 2023, CMC University announced the “CMC – Because You Deserve It” scholarship worth 92 billion VND, covering 50% – 70% – 100% of the entire course tuition fees (excluding English language tuition). The university also guarantees 100% employment at CMC Group and Samsung for bilingual technology program students enrolling in the academic year 2023.

Parallel to the scholarship and employment programs, CMC University also commits to enhancing the learning experience and creating a high-quality, advanced educational environment for its students. As a university within a corporation, CMC University continuously strengthens the links between the institution and businesses to improve the quality of practical training programs, offering professional experiences and job opportunities for students.

CMC University’s training programs are designed to international standards with 100% imported curricula from renowned global publishers. Specifically, the Information Technology program is aligned with the ABET accreditation standards and ITSS-IP/FE certification; the Business Administration program is guided by AACSB standards; the Japanese Language program follows the JLPT N2 certification, and the Korean Language program is aligned with TOPIK 4 certification.
