1. Results of the Proficiency Assessment Exams (HSA) organized by Hanoi National University in 2022 are considered satisfactory if achieved 80 points or higher.

Results of the Proficiency Assessment Exams organized by Ho Chi Minh City National University in 2022 are considered satisfactory if achieved 650 points or higher.

Results of the Cognitive Assessment Exam organized by the University of Science and Technology – Hanoi (Bach Khoa) are considered satisfactory if achieved 15 points or higher (K01 or K02 combination).

Calculation method for admission evaluation based on high school academic performance (transcript).

Admission assessment is based on the overall average scores of three semesters (Semester 1 and Semester 2 in grade 11, and Semester 1 in grade 12). The formula for calculating the admission score:

Admission Score = Subject 1 Average + Subject 2 Average + Subject 3 Average

(Subject average is calculated as follows: Subject 1 Average = (Grade 11 Subject 1 Average + Grade 12 Semester 1 Subject 1 Average) / 2)
