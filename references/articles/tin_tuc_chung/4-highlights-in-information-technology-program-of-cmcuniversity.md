## 4 Highlights in Information Technology program of CMC University

The Information Technology (IT) program at CMC University provides students with knowledge, skills, and high competitiveness upon graduation, while also supporting the attainment of international certifications. Here are four highlights of the Information Technology program at CMC University:

The Information Technology program at CMC University is designed in accordance with the standards of the Association for Computing Machinery (ACM) and the Information Technology Skill Standard (ITSS) of Japan. ITSS is a Japanese IT skill certification developed through collaboration between the Ministry of Science and Technology and the Ministry of Economy, Trade, and Industry of Japan, starting in 2021.

In addition to ACM standards, the CMC University IT program supplements knowledge areas required for ITSS certification, such as hardware, management science, accounting, and intellectual property ownership. The Department of Information Technology and Communication also provides study materials for ITSS in Japanese, English, and Vietnamese, allowing students additional resources for reference and research.

Leveraging its position as a leading technology member in Vietnam, CMC University confidently guarantees 100% employment at CMC Technology Group and Samsung for students in bilingual technology programs entering the 2023 academic year. After graduation, students in technology-related fields can apply to various units within CMC Technology Group, an entity with a substantial annual demand for skilled personnel. The university and the corporation also facilitate connections, providing opportunities for students to work in international environments such as Japan, South Korea, and the United States.

Starting in 2023, CMC University officially enrolls and educates students in the bilingual Information Technology program, with 100% of specialized courses taught entirely in English. The bilingual education program is specially designed for students proficient in English, enabling them to study specialized subjects in the language. Apart from foundational courses, all specialized courses in the bilingual program will be conducted in English.

The English language proficiency requirement for the bilingual program at CMC University is IELTS 5.5 or equivalent. In cases where students do not have an IELTS 5.5 certificate or equivalent, they will undergo an English proficiency test and be placed in corresponding language levels. After completing Level 6 of the university’s English program, students can proceed to the first semester of the main program.

In 2023, CMC University awards the “CMC – Because You Deserve” Scholarship Fund valued at 92 billion VND to students with outstanding academic achievements. The scholarship provides opportunities for recipients to receive 50%, 70%, or 100% of the total course fees.

CMC University, with the school code CMC, plans to admit 1,300 undergraduate students in 2023 for majors including Information Technology, Computer Science, Business Administration, Graphic Design, Japanese Language, and Korean Language.
