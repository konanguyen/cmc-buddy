## CMC University Recruitment Announcement for 2022

To meet its developmental needs, CMC University is seeking dedicated, talented, and qualified candidates to join in building a pioneering digital university. The available positions are as follows:

#### Positions Available:

Head of Student Affairs;

Student Affairs Specialist;

Head of Human Resources;

Human Resources Specialist (Recruitment);

Training Specialist;

Administrative Staff – Records and Archives;

Administrative Staff – Receptionist;

Business Administration Lecturer;

Information Technology Lecturer;

English Lecturer.

#### Work Location:

Campus 1 – CMC University, 84 Nguyen Thanh Binh, Ha Dong, Hanoi.

#### Benefits:

Dynamic and professional working environment with career advancement opportunities;

Competitive salary, negotiable based on capabilities;

Opportunities for participation in advanced training activities;

12 annual leave days and 1 paid birthday leave day;

Company-provided 24-hour insurance as per the policy for employees, along with state-regulated social, health, and unemployment insurance;

Tuition fee discounts for family members studying at CMC University;

Other benefits include participation in team-building programs, annual vacation trips, recreational activities, etc.
