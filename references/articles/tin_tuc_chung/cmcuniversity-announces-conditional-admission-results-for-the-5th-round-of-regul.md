## 4. Candidates who have been conditionally admitted must meet both of the following conditions:

Complete high school or equivalent program (according to the 2023 university admission regulations of the Ministry of Education and Training).

## 5. Regulations regarding enrollment fees

Enrollment fee is the amount candidates pay to reserve a place in the course after being conditionally admitted but haven’t officially completed the enrollment procedure.

Applicable applicants:Candidates conditionally admitted to CMC University in 2023 but haven’t officially completed the enrollment procedure.

Enrollment fee amount:5,000,000 VND.

Enrollment fee regulations:If candidates enroll at CMC University, this fee will be considered a pre-payment and deducted from the first tuition fee payment when they officially enroll at the University. If candidates do not enroll at CMC University, this fee is non-refundable.

## 6. Policies for candidates submitting applications early and preference ranking

Candidates conditionally admitted in Round 5 and pay the enrollment fee before July 10, 2023, receive a 20% tuition fee discount for the first year (semesters 1, 2, 3), excluding English language tuition fees (if applicable). This policy doesn’t apply if candidates are eligible for higher scholarships or benefits.

To officially confirm conditional admission to CMC University, candidates meeting the admission requirements for early admission to CMC University must register and prioritize their first choice on the Ministry of Education and Training’s admission portal at July 10 to 5:00 PM on July 30, 2023.

If candidates do not register their desired preferences that meet the admission criteria (excluding high school graduation requirements) on the Ministry of Education and Training’s admission portal, it means they waive their right to this admission.
