#### About CMC University

CMC University is a young university with a spirit of innovation, invested by the CMC Corporation to develop towards advanced technological and engineering education and pioneering digital education in Vietnam. The goal is to become a reputable global university by 2043. The university’s mission is to create knowledge, train high-quality human resources in the fields of science, technology, and innovation, contributing to the development of a strong Vietnam.

Following the principles of liberal education and education for life, the university is a place where faculty, staff, and students engage in creativity, learning, research, work, personal development, and social service. The working environment is built and standardized according to international norms. Additionally, faculty and students have the opportunity to participate in applied research projects, technology development, and entrepreneurship within the CMC Corporation’s business ecosystem – investment fund – research institute – university.
