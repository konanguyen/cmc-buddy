## CMC University warmly welcomed hundreds of high school students to the “CMC University’s Experienced Day: Unbox Your Future” event.

On March 12, 2023, CMC University warmly hosted an event called “CMC University’s Experienced Day: Unbox Your Future” at the Vạn Phúc campus. The event aimed to provide high school students with the opportunity to explore the facilities and experience the digital university environment as authentic university students, helping them make informed decisions about their future career paths. The program, which offered vivid and immersive experiences, attracted hundreds of high school students from various schools in Hanoi.

### Exploring CMC University’s Modern Facilities – The First Digital University Model in Vietnam

At the “CMC University’s Experienced Day: Unbox Your Future” event, hundreds of high school students had the chance to personally visit and explore the learning and practical environments, as well as the living spaces of CMC University students. Stepping foot into the pioneering digital university, these high school students were thrilled to experience new things and were introduced to the modern facilities and “made by CMC” amenities available to CMC University students. The enthusiastic and friendly guidance provided by the “guides,” including faculty members, lecturers, and current CMC University students, left a lasting and positive impression on the visiting high school students.

Participating in Real Student Experience Workshops

The high school students didn’t just stop at touring the physical facilities. They also had the opportunity to actively participate in workshops focused on developing soft skills in the digital era. Soft skills training is a distinctive feature of CMC University’s educational programs. To help students meet the future labor market’s requirements, incorporating soft skills into the curriculum has become an essential element in all fields of study at CMC University.

The first lessons for a new CMC University student include self-awareness, emotional intelligence enhancement, creativity, critical thinking, and respect for differences. Participating in standard 45-minute classes with lively group discussions, these high school students had the chance to become real students without having to wait until they are officially enrolled in university.

Confidently Becoming Global Citizens through the Talkshow “Gen Z – Experiencing and Maturing in the Digital Era”

To help high school students choose careers that align with their passions and offer opportunities in the digital era, the “CMC University’s Experienced Day: Unbox Your Future” event featured the talkshow “Gen Z – Experiencing and Maturing in the Digital Era.” During this session, high school students had the opportunity to meet and engage openly with speakers representing two generations, Gen Y and Gen Z: Mr. Đặng Tùng Sơn, Deputy CEO of CMC Telecom; Mr. Hồ Như Hải, Deputy Rector of CMC University; and Ms. Vũ Linh Nga, an outstanding student majoring in Information Technology & Communication, who received a 100% scholarship for the entire 2022 academic year.

The talkshow provided valuable advice for Gen Z on self-development, creating a learning path for experiencing and maturing in the digital era, and becoming global citizens. “At the age of 18, seize the opportunity to design your own future journey. Show maturity by choosing your own profession, major, and university and persuading your parents to invest in your choices,” as quoted by Mr. Hồ Như Hải, Deputy Rector of CMC University, addressed to the high school students when they stand at the threshold of choosing their future career paths.

According to representatives from CMC University’s leadership, university education is a journey of “self-learning” and “experiencing.” Therefore, high school students should choose an educational environment that fosters self-learning and provides them with the most opportunities for experiences. Experience helps individuals grow and discover their true value. In addition, high school students gained insights into the dynamic and exciting life of CMC University students, their academic stories, and experiences in the pioneering digital university environment through the sharing session by Vũ Linh Nga during the talkshow.

In addition to valuable career and university selection advice, high school students also listened to real-world experiences about working in the digital era from an Information Technology expert’s perspective, shared by Mr. Đặng Tùng Sơn, Deputy CEO of CMC Telecom.

Especially, at the event, Vũ Linh Nga officially received an internship offer from the Deputy CEO of CMC Telecom. This serves as evidence that CMC Technology Corporation’s member companies always welcome CMC University students to participate in internships during their studies and are ready to offer job opportunities after graduation. According to the Deputy CEO of CMC Telecom, welcoming CMC University students for internships is part of the company’s human resource development and expansion strategy in the near future. CMC Telecom will create the best conditions for students to learn and develop themselves, ensuring their success in their future careers.

At the conclusion of the program, lucky high school students in the Lucky Draw segment received meaningful gifts from CMC University, presented by Mr. Hồ Như Hải, Deputy Rector of the university.
