Recruitment Position: Data Analytics Lecturer

Job Description

Develop teaching plans, design, and update lecture content for one or more subjects related to data analysis specialization.

Teach data analysis courses including Business Analytics, Data Analysis in Business, Business Data Visualization, Probability and Statistics Theory, and other specialized data analysis subjects.

Conduct both theoretical and practical teaching in class or online as per the school’s schedule and regulations.

Participate in professional development activities, curriculum development, supervise theses, oversee examinations, prepare exam questions, grade exams, etc.

Engage in scientific research as per the university’s regulations.

Perform other tasks as assigned by the Department Head and as directed by the Principal.

Requirements

Degree: Master’s/Ph.D. in Data Science/Data Analysis/Business Analyst/Business Intelligence/Business Administration, preferably from a foreign university.

Experience: Teaching experience in data analysis courses is an advantage, practical experience in a data analysis role in the business sector.

Ability to read and research materials in English.

Good pedagogical and communication skills.

Passionate, dedicated, and enthusiastic about teaching, familiar with teaching methods like active learning, problem-based learning, and case teaching.

Enthusiastic, committed, and enjoys working with students.
