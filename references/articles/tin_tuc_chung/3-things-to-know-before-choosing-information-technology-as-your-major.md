## 3 Things to Know Before Choosing Information Technology as Your Major

Information Technology is a rapidly growing field with high demand for skilled professionals. Talented students have many opportunities as businesses actively seek them when they are still at university.

Before choosing Information Technology as a major at the university level, prospective students need to explore the field, understand the necessary skills, and select a quality learning environment.

#### Learn About Fields and Specializations in Information Technology

Information Technology (IT) is a field that uses computers to create, process, store, retrieve, and exchange all kinds of data and information. IT is commonly used in business activities rather than personal or entertainment technologies and is part of Information and Communication Technology (ICT).

Information Technology encompasses various fields, each with numerous different specializations. Each field or specialization has its own specific knowledge and is related to various professional areas.

One prominent specialization in IT is computer engineering, a branch of electrical engineering that integrates computer science with electronic engineering to develop hardware and software. Computer engineers are often trained in electronic engineering, software design, and the integration of hardware and software, rather than focusing solely on software or electronic engineering.

Information technology comprises many fields with diverse specializations.

Students can study Information Systems (IS), a platform designed to collect, process, store, and distribute information. Data from these systems are used to provide information, contribute to knowledge, and create digital products.

Artificial Intelligence (AI) is rapidly advancing. AI is a research field involving intelligent agents, referring to machine systems capable of perceiving environmental factors and taking actions to maximize goal achievement. Data science is an interdisciplinary field related to data mining, machine learning, and big data. It employs methods, processes, algorithms, and scientific systems to extract knowledge and applies it in various fields.

The Information Security specialization aims to protect information by minimizing risks. Graduates in this field can become network administrators, system administrators, system engineers, database administrators, computer security experts, and computer engineers.

CMC University (formerly known as Asia-Pacific College of Technology and Innovation, CMC) – invested by CMC Corporation – specializes in training prominent majors in the IT industry such as Software Engineering, Information Security, Artificial Intelligence and Data Science, Computer Networks, Information Systems, and Computer Engineering.
