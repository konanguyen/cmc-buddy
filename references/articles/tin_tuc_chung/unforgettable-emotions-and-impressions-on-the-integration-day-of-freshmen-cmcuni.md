## Unforgettable Emotions and Impressions on the Integration Day of Freshmen – CMC University, Cohort 2, 2023

From August 29th to August 31st, the orientation event for the freshmen admitted in the first round of 2023 at CMC University took place in an atmosphere of excitement and vibrancy. Beyond completing the necessary enrollment procedures before the new academic year, Cohort 2 freshmen preserved numerous emotions and unforgettable impressions during this significant event, marking a major milestone in their lives.

The atmosphere was bustling and exciting on the day of welcoming new students of Cohort 2 – CMC University to complete the admission procedures.

Early in the morning on the enrollment days, even before the enrollment procedures officially began, the atmosphere at CMC University was lively and bustling. Hundreds of freshmen and their parents from various provinces gathered with the desire to quickly complete the enrollment procedures, explore the campus, and learn more about the university.

On the first day of welcoming Cohort 2 freshmen, Prof. Dr. Nguyen Ngoc Binh, the President of CMC University, addressed the crowd: “On behalf of the University, I warmly welcome and congratulate parents and freshmen present at CMC University today. It is an honor to welcome esteemed parents and freshmen. In recent days, many parents and students have come to CMC University to prepare for this important event—the official enrollment day for freshmen.”

Especially on the first day of enrollment, Prof. Dr. Nguyen Ngoc Binh, the President of CMC University, presented a bouquet of flowers to congratulate Tran Khanh Linh, a freshman majoring in Information Technology and her family. It is worth noting that Tran Khanh Linh, from Son La province, was the first to arrive at the enrollment location at 4:30 am on August 29th.

Associate Professor. Dr. Nguyen Ngoc Binh – Principal of CMC University presented a fresh bouquet of flowers to congratulate Tran Khanh Linh – a new student majoring in Information Technology and her family.

A large number of new students and parents gathered at CMC University early in the morning to complete procedures and prepare for the new school year.

The smiles, excited expressions, or the confused and innocent emotions of the students who entered the university classroom for the first time and with the mindset of being new students have become beautiful memories.

New students are excited and eager to experience an interesting university environment in the near future.

Prior to enrollment, the enrollment procedures were communicated through various channels of CMC University. Thanks to the enthusiastic support and guidance from the faculty, lecturers, and volunteer students, parents, and freshmen quickly completed the enrollment procedures according to the prescribed process. On the enrollment day, parents and freshmen were also provided with thoughtful advice on information about civilized, safe, and convenient dormitory areas around the campus to assist those freshmen who come from afar in settling into their accommodations.

Guided by the faculty and volunteer students, Cohort 2 “rookies” quickly completed the enrollment procedures according to the prescribed process.

With warm hospitality, visits to functional rooms and classrooms, conversations with lecturers to learn brief information about their majors, Cohort 2 freshmen approached the new academic journey with joy and hope. Bright faces, shining with joy and excitement before the new school year when they become new students at CMC University, mark an important turning point in their lives when entering university.

Mixed with the joy and excitement of the new students, it is not difficult to see the kind, warm and proud eyes of the parents who always follow each step of the new students. Happiness shines on every parent’s face when they see their children gradually growing up and becoming mature students. Perhaps, the journey of new students to reach the university door is not only their own aspirations, but also continuing their parents’ dreams.

The trusting eyes of the parents followed every step of the new students of Course 2.

Moreover, it wasn’t just a time for completing procedures and preparing for the new academic year; the enrollment day for Cohort 2 freshmen also left many memorable moments. One particularly special gesture was the gift from the family of Nguyen Viet Ha, the top student in the Business Administration major (School of Business and Management), presented to CMC University on the morning of August 30th.

The family of Nguyen Viet Ha – Valedictorian of the Business Administration major (Faculty of Business & Management) took a commemorative photo with representatives of the Board of Directors and staff of CMC University.

Accompanying the Business Administration major freshman on the enrollment day, Nguyen Viet Ha’s family gave a meaningful gift, a clock, to CMC University. With this meaningful gift, Mr. Nguyen Van Bao, Nguyen Viet Ha’s grandfather, shared: “The family is presenting this clock as a wish to CMC University, hoping that the university will develop and endure over time.” It is noteworthy that Mr. Nguyen Van Bao, Nguyen Viet Ha’s grandfather, is a former lecturer at the Artillery Officer School, Hanoi.

Especially, there were freshmen who were close friends choosing CMC University as their final destination, and there were even more special pairs. That is the story of two pairs of twins—Doan Ha Gia Bach and Doan Ha Chi Bach, and Hoang Tung Anh and Hoang Duy Anh. It is known that these two special pairs of twins officially registered to enroll in the Japanese Language major at CMC University with expectations and excitement for their new academic journey.

Twin brothers Doan Ha Gia Bach and Doan Ha Chi Bach – new students of cohort 2 of Japanese Language major, CMC University

Twins Hoang Tung Anh and Hoang Duy Anh both chose to pursue the Japanese Language major at CMC University.

The enrollment day for Cohort 2 freshmen at CMC University has officially come to an end, leaving a mark in the hearts of every parent and freshman with a wide range of emotions. Once again, welcome all freshmen to CMC University, and we wish you beautiful, passionate, and memorable years at the common home of CMC University.

Let’s look back at the memorable moments of the enrollment day for Cohort 2 freshmen at CMC University!
