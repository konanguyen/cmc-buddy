## Cozy atmosphere of Vietnam Teacher’s Day Anniversary on November 20 at the event “CMC EDUCATOR’S DAY 2023 – THE INSPIRER”

Immersed in a joyful and exciting atmosphere echoing nationwide to commemorate the 41st anniversary of Vietnam Teacher’s Day (November 20, 1982 – November 20, 2023), in the morning of November 20, CMC Technology Corporation and CMC University solemnly organized the event “CMC EDUCATOR’S DAY 2023 – THE INSPIRER.” The event aims to express gratitude and honor the contributions to the noble education of the entire lecturers and staff of CMC University and the training officers from CMC Technology Corporation.

The event was honored to have the presence of distinguished guests from CMC Technology Corporation including: Mr. Nguyen Trung Chinh – Chairman of the Board, CMC Technology Corporation, Chairman of the CMC University Council; Mr. Le Thanh Son – Senior Vice Chairman, Director of Finance of CMC Technology Corporation; Mr. Ho Thanh Tung – CEO of CMC Technology Corporation, along with internal training officers and lecturers of the Corporation.

From CMC University, there was the participation of Assoc. Prof. Dr. Nguyen Tuong Vy – Vice Rector; Assoc. Prof. Dr. Nguyen Thanh Tung – Vice Rector; Mr. Ho Nhu Hai – Vice Rector, along with all lecturers, staff, and students of CMC University.

Speaking at the ceremony, Mr. Nguyen Trung Chinh – Chairman of the Board, CMC Technology Corporation, Chairman of the CMC University Council, extended his warmest congratulations to the entire lecturers and staff of the university, as well as the training officers within the Corporation.

“Despite only entering its second year, CMC University has achieved remarkablely proud milestones. Not only did we successfully admit 1,000 students, but we also completed two training campuses with modern and luxurious facilities. We are gradually building a digital university model, taking the first steps on the journey to affirm the university’s position in the Vietnamese higher education system. To achieve such results, we cannot overlook the contribution of the University Council, the Board of Directors, and especially the entire team of officers and lecturers who tirelessly devoted themselves day and night to build the university. On Vietnam Teacher’s Day, November 20, on behalf of the University Council, I would like to express sincere thanks to all the teachers for their tireless efforts in the past year, which enables us to achieve encouraging results today,” quoted from the speech of Mr. Nguyen Trung Chinh – Chairman of the Board of Directors of CMC Technology Corporation, Chairman of the Council of CMC University at the event.

At the event, Assoc. Prof. Dr. Nguyen Tuong Vy – Vice Principle of CMC University, delivered a speech: “Dear students, take pride in studying in the unique environment of CMC University – a university within the heart of the enterprise, where you are trained in connection with the practical aspects at CMC Technology Corporation and its partners. We always express deep gratitude to all the teachers for their significant contributions to the prosperity of the national education. Each teacher is a powerful source of motivation, continuously driving students to strive and excel.”

Also at the event, CMC University announced and issued decisions on the appointment of managerial positions within the university, including: Assoc. Prof. Dr. Vu Viet Vu taking on the position of Head of the Department of Information Technology & Telecommunications; Ms. Nguyen Tra My assuming the position of Deputy Head of the Department of Business and Management; Dr. Nguyen Minh Kien taking on the position of Deputy Head of the Department of Fine Arts & Design.

On this special occasion, CMC Technology Corporation honored the internal faculty members and training officers who have made significant contributions to the training and development activities of the corporation in 2023. Additionally, they expressed gratitude by presenting tokens of appreciation to the Board of Directors and exemplary lecturers of CMC University.

As one of the outstanding educators actively involved in teaching and scientific research, inspiring both faculty and students in the Department of Business and Management, Mr. Nguyen Tra My excellently achieved the title of “The Inspirational Trainer.” The award acknowledges her contributions to the development of the training program.

Especially, in the final part of the program, CMC University students and CMC Technology Corporation staff expressed their wishes and gratitude to all the teachers and colleagues working in the field of education and training. These heartfelt wishes were personally written and hung on a special appreciation tree by the students and staff. These are symbolic gifts that serve as spiritual support, providing additional motivation for educators to inspire and disseminate knowledge to more learners, contributing to the overall enhancement of capabilities for CMC Technology Corporation staff and, specifically, CMC University students.
