## CMC University Recruitment Announcement for Admissions Staff

#### Position: Admissions Consulting Specialist.

Number of Positions:1.

Job Description:

Contact, advise, and assist candidates in understanding detailed information about the university, choosing schools and majors through various online and offline methods.

Resolve candidates’ queries regarding training programs, majors, and admission information.

Responsible for data entry and compiling admission-related reports.

Participate in admissions activities as scheduled.

Assist in events and activities related to admissions counseling (roadshows, open days, etc.).

Candidate Requirements:

Bachelor’s degree in a relevant field.

Female candidates preferred.

Minimum 6 months to 1 year of experience in customer service or client consultation in the service industry.

Candidates with experience in admissions consulting or working in an educational environment are preferred.

Proficient in office software.

Good communication and persuasion skills, understanding customer psychology.

Ability to work under high pressure.

Good health, able to travel as required.

Pleasant appearance, clear speech.

Hardworking, honest, careful, enthusiastic, friendly attitude, responsible and committed to the job.

Benefits for Candidates:

Competitive income (monthly salary + bonus).

Full participation in social, health, and unemployment insurance as per labor laws.

Professional, dynamic work environment with enthusiastic colleagues, opportunities for advancement and development.

Company and group holidays bonus.

Other benefits: annual vacation trips, participation in recreational activities.

Application Information:

Email: tuyensinh@cmc-u.edu.vn

Hotline: 024 7102 9999 or 0865 000 218.

#### Position: Admissions Officer (Promoter).

Participate in planning year-round, monthly, and weekly roadshow activities for admissions, aligned with the university’s admissions strategy and goals.

Generate creative ideas and execute promotional activities for admissions directly targeting potential customers.

Build and maintain relationships with partners in university admissions, especially high schools and Departments of Education and Training.

Implement roadshow promotional activities in assigned high schools.

Organize events at CMC University, high schools, or other venues to attract candidates and parents.

Conduct consulting activities in tours organized by media agencies or groups of schools in different provinces.

Organize other events serving admissions.

Measure the success of organized roadshow activities.

Provide consultation to candidates and parents, in person or via phone.

Manage candidate profiles, encourage candidates to complete admission applications.

Research the market and target customers to develop effective solutions for roadshows and events.

Undertake other admissions-related tasks as assigned by the Admissions Manager.

Male candidates preferred.

#### Position: Admissions Collaborator (Fulltime).

Number of Positions:5.

Assist in consulting and addressing inquiries from students about CMC University’s academic programs.

Support in preparing for admissions seminars, consulting programs, and career guidance in Hanoi and neighboring provinces.

Guide students and parents on application processes and enrollment documentation.

Perform other tasks as assigned by the department head.

Target Group: Final-year students or graduates from colleges and universities in Hanoi.

Female, aged 20 – 25 years.

Engaging voice, clear speech, no speech impediments.

Good communication skills, effective listening and persuasion skills.

Candidates with experience in Telesales, consulting, and customer service are preferred.

Willing to travel to provinces and cities.

Professional and comfortable working environment, beneficial for future CV development.

Work in a dynamic, professional environment with opportunities for career development.

Regular training and skill development.

Annual performance evaluations and potential for official contract renewal.
