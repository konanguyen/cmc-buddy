### About CMC Technology Group and CMC University

Founded in 1993, CMC Technology Group is a leading IT and telecommunications enterprise in Vietnam. Its ecosystem includes Technology & Solution, Global Business, Telecommunications, and Research and Education sectors. CMC has 7 member companies & joint ventures, an applied technology research institute, and a university.

CMC University, a member of CMC Technology Group, is developed as an advanced technological and digital university, aiming to be a world-renowned Vietnamese university by 2043. Its mission is to create knowledge and train high-quality human resources in science, technology, and innovation, contributing to the development of a powerful Vietnam.

The Department of Business and Management aspires to become a prestigious international business school, training responsible, technologically adept, flexible, adaptive, and creative business leaders and managers for the international and digital era. The department’s programs aim for ACBSP international accreditation.

Currently, the department offers a bachelor’s program in Business Administration with five majors.
