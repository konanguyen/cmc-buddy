## CMC University announces the scholarship assessment results for the fifth round, intended for students admitted to the regular undergraduate program in 2023.

## Scholarship Information:

CMC Liberation Scholarship: Covers 100% of the tuition fees for the entire course.

CMC Innovation Scholarship: Covers 70% of the tuition fees for the entire course.

CMC Pioneer Scholarship: Covers 50% of the tuition fees for the entire course.

For detailed information about the 2023 Scholarship and Financial Support Policy, visit: here.

## Eligible Applicants:

Students admitted to CMC University who participated in the fifth-round scholarship assessment using one of the early admission methods in 2023:

Admission based on high school academic results (transcript) according to the subject combination for admission.

Direct admission according to the regulations of the Ministry of Education and the specific regulations of CMC University.

## Scholarship Assessment Criteria:

Applicants must meet at least 01 scholarship assessment criterion. Details can be viewed at: here.

Scholarship allocation will prioritize applicants based on their assessment results from high to low (favoring applicants who submitted their admission applications early).

To confirm the CMC University scholarship, successful applicants admitted to the University need to submit the enrollment fee and prioritize CMC as preferences 1, 2, or 3 in the General Admission System of the Ministry of Education and Training. For detailed information on enrollment fees, visit: here.

If applicants who meet all admission conditions (except high school graduation requirements) do not register their preferences at the Ministry of Education and Training’s Admissions Portal, it signifies their waiver of admission and this scholarship result.
