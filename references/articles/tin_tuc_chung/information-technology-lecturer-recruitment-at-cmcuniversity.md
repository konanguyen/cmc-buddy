## Information Technology Lecturer Recruitment at CMC University

Position:Information Technology (IT) Lecturer, Department of IT and Communications.

Salary:Negotiable.

Job Description:

a) Teaching:

Teach subjects in areas like Software Engineering, Information Security, Artificial Intelligence and Data Science, Information Systems, and/or Computer Engineering.

Guide students in projects, assignments, and internships.

Participate in various training activities and educational boards.

b) Research:

Fundraise and secure sponsorship for scientific research.

Conduct research and publish scientific results.

c) Other Professional and Community Service Tasks:

Academic advising for students.

Consulting on student projects (scientific research, entrepreneurship, community, etc.).

Participate in student management and university administration.

Community service.

Qualifications:

Master’s degree or higher in a relevant field.

Profound knowledge of assigned subjects; general knowledge of related subjects in the assigned department.

Ability to use IT and English language in teaching and research.

Respect CMC Uni’s core values of Liberalism, Creativity, Practicality, Critical Thinking, and Respect for Diversity.

Candidates with more than 02 years of university teaching experience and/or significant scientific research achievements are preferred.

Working Conditions:

Academic year comprises 48 working weeks.

Annual teaching hours are 330, with the possibility to teach between 135 to 540 hours based on capability and desire.

Remaining time in the academic year dedicated to research, other professional tasks, and community service.

Reasons to Choose CMC Uni:

An open and humanistic work environment that values faculty voices.

State-of-the-art digital infrastructure, LMS, and equipment for teaching and student interaction.

Competitive income based on capabilities and work efficiency.

Participation in the CMC Uni Faculty Development Program (training in flipped classroom models, blended learning methods, and experiential learning methods like Team-based Learning, Project-based Learning, Action-based Learning, etc.).

Opportunities for personal career development in an ecosystem of Education & Training – Research – Business.

Application Submission:

Candidates should send their Application Letter and CV via email to hr.edu@cmc.com.vn with the email subject “CMCU_IT Lecturer”.

The application should include an Application Letter and CV (Curriculum Vitae).
