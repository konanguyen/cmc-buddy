## CMC University has officially become a member of the Association of Vietnamese Universities and Colleges.

On June 5, CMC University was formally admitted and became a member of the Association of Vietnamese Universities and Colleges.

The certificate acknowledging CMC University as an official member of the Association of Vietnamese Universities and Colleges is dated June 5, 2023

According to the decision, CMC University holds the rights and responsibilities as stipulated in the association’s regulations, effective from June 5, 2023.

This official membership is a significant step in the sustainable development of CMC University.

With the support of the Association of Vietnamese Universities and Colleges, CMC University will actively participate in activities aimed at contributing to the development and enhancement of the quality of education within the association. Beyond fulfilling its mission alongside the association, CMC University will further promote collaboration with domestic and international organizations to exchange experiences in education, scientific research, application, technology transfer, workshops, conferences, study tours, volunteer exchanges, faculty and student exchanges, counseling, and training services.

The Association of Vietnamese Universities and Colleges is a professional social organization of Vietnamese universities and colleges, comprising Vietnamese citizens. It was established to unite and solidify its members, protect their legitimate rights and interests, support each other in effective activities, and contribute to the development of the education and training sector, meeting the socio-economic development needs of the country. With over 11 years of establishment and development, the association has successfully organized numerous seminars, conferences, and discussions to accurately assess the situation and understand the development trends in regional and global education. It has provided accurate opinions to promote the country’s higher education innovation.

Furthermore, the Association of Vietnamese Universities and Colleges has contributed significant insights on issues related to higher education innovation, particularly in private education, and education and training in general. This contribution aids the Party’s guidance, government management, and the Ministry of Education and Training’s decision-making by providing authentic information and advice.
