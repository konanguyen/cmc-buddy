## Announcement of the Scholarship Review Schedule for the First Round of 2022

The Scholarship Review Board of CMC University (formerly known as Asia-Pacific Industrial Fine Arts University) announces the schedule for the review and awarding of scholarships for the first round of 2022 as follows:

## 1. Scholarships:

CMC Liberation Scholarship:covers 100% of the entire course tuition fee (equivalent to 198-234 million VND per slot, depending on the field of study).

CMC Innovation Scholarship:covers 70% of the entire course tuition fee.

CMC Pioneer Scholarship:covers 50% of the entire course tuition fee.

Details regarding the 2022 Scholarship Policies and Financial Support can be found here: Link to Scholarship Policies: .

## 2. Eligible Applicants:

Applicants who have applied for scholarships at CMC University and have been admitted to the university through one of the following methods:

Admitted based on high school academic results (CMC200).

Direct admission according to CMC University’s admission plan (CMC303).

Admitted based on the 2022 National Aptitude Assessment at Hanoi National University, Ho Chi Minh City National University, or the Cognitive Assessment at Hanoi University of Science and Technology (CMC402).

Applicants who have registered for the scholarship and have applied to CMC University through the method of admission based on the results of the 2022 National High School Graduation Examination (CMC100).
