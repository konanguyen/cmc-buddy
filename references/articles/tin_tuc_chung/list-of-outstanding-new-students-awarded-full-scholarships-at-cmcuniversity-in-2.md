Furthermore, during the inauguration ceremony of CMC University – the First Digital University in Vietnam & the Opening of the First Academic Year held on November 17, 2022, the top 10 students with outstanding achievements and high scores in the 2022 High School Graduation Exam were also honored.

“I also sincerely congratulate the parents and relatives of these students on their initial achievements, overcoming the challenging and stressful admission period, fulfilling the family’s hopes and wishes! The university expects you, the students, to continue striving for academic excellence, to persevere and improve yourselves. You are shouldering a noble mission as the first-year students of CMC University, contributing to building the traditions of the university, and being honored as students of an advanced university, the first of its kind in Vietnam.” – Assoc. Prof. Nguyễn Ngọc Bình, President of CMC University, congratulates the outstanding scholarship recipients and extends a message to all first-year students at CMC University during the ceremony.

With the sponsorship of CMC Technology Corporation, CMC University awards the ‘CMC – Because You Deserve It’ scholarship fund with a total value of up to 69 billion VND for talented students who have excellent academic achievements and are enrolling in 2022. The scholarship program includes categories such as CMC Liberal, CMC Pioneer, and CMC Innovation, providing support with values ranging from 50% to 100% of the entire course tuition fees, aiming to assist students in their educational journey.
