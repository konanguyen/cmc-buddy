#### About CMC Technology Group and CMC University

Founded in 1993, CMC Technology Group is a leading IT and telecommunications company in Vietnam. Its ecosystem comprises the Technology & Solution, Global Business, Telecommunications, and Research and Education sectors. CMC has 7 member companies & joint ventures, an applied technology research institute, and a university.

CMC University, a member of the CMC Technology Group, is developed as an advanced technological and digital university, with a vision to become a world-renowned Vietnamese university by 2043. Its mission is to create knowledge and train high-quality human resources in the fields of science, technology, and innovation, contributing to building a powerful Vietnam.

The Department of Information Technology and Communications aims to develop top-ranked IT and Telecommunications programs regionally and globally by 2033 and 2043. It focuses on training high-quality human resources with social responsibility, professional competence, creativity, flexibility, and management skills for success in the international and digital era. The department’s programs are oriented towards international ABET accreditation.

Currently, the department offers two bachelor’s programs in Information Technology and Computer Science with seven majors.
