## CMC University Announces Supplemental Round Admission Scores for 2023

The Admissions Council of CMC University has announced the supplemental round admission scores for 2023 for various methods including basing on national high school graduation exam results, evaluation of high school academic records, and direct admissions as per the regulations of the Ministry of Education and Training and the University’s Admissions Plan.

For direct admissions, candidates who meet all the direct admission criteria as stipulated in the regulations of the Ministry of Education and Training and the University’s 2023 Admissions Plan are eligible for admission.

Candidates can check their supplemental round admission results for CMC University in 2023 at the website: these steps:

Step 1: Access[].

Step 2: Candidates check the official admission results in the “Admission Notice Inquiry” section.

Step 3: Enter ID card number and click “View Results”.

As per regulations, by 5:00 PM on September 8, 2023, all candidates who receive admission results to CMC University must confirm their enrollment online in the first round on the Ministry of Education and Training’s admissions system. Failure to complete this process will be considered as forfeiture of university admission.

The Admissions Council of CMC University will send admission notices and enrollment instructions to candidates from August 23, 2023. Parents and students are advised to regularly check the University’s website for the latest updates.
