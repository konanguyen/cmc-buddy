## CMC University announces the results and awards of the 6th scholarship round for students admitted to the regular undergraduate program in 2023.

The scholarship review and granting council of CMC University (school code: CMC) announces the results of “CMC – Because you deserve it” scholarship, round 6 for students admitted to the full-time university system in 2023 as follows:

CMC Liberation Scholarship:Covers 100% of the tuition fees for the entire course.

CMC Innovation Scholarship:Covers 70% of the tuition fees for the entire course.

CMC Pioneer Scholarship:Covers 50% of the tuition fees for the entire course.

For detailed scholarship policies and financial support for 2023, please refer to: Scholarship Policy and Financial Support.

The scholarship applies to candidates who have been admitted to the University of CMC and participated in the 6th scholarship evaluation through one of the following admission methods in 2023:

Admission based on the results of the 2023 high school graduation exam.

Admission based on high school academic performance (school records) according to the subject combination for admission.

Direct admission according to the regulations of the Ministry of Education and Training and the University of CMC.

Candidates must meet at least 01 scholarship evaluation criterion. For detailed information, visit: Scholarship Policy and Financial Support.

Based on the admission results, candidates will be ranked from highest to lowest (priority given to candidates who submitted their applications early).

To confirm the scholarship, admitted candidates need to confirm their enrollment at the University of CMC through the Ministry of Education and Training’s centralized admission system from August 24 to 5:00 PM on September 08, 2023.

Instructions to Check Scholarship Results:

Step 1:Visit the website:

Step 2:Select the “Check Scholarships” tab.

Step 3:Enter the ID card number or citizen identification card number used for the admission application.

From August 24 to 5:00 PM on September 08, 2023, failure to confirm enrollment at the eligible admitted choice (excluding high school graduation condition) through the Ministry of Education and Training’s centralized admission system implies the candidate’s withdrawal from admission rights and forfeiture of the scholarship.
