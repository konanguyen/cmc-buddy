## Announcement of the Results of the 2022 Associate Professor Appointment Review

Pursuant to Decision No. 37/2018/QĐ-TTg dated August 31, 2018 of the Prime Minister promulgating regulations on the criteria, procedures for reviewing and recognizing the attainment of the standards and appointing the titles of professor and associate professor; procedures for reviewing the cancellation of recognition of the title and dismissal of the title of professor and associate professor;

On December 19, 2022 and December 21, 2022, the Review Committee for the Appointment of Professor and Associate Professor in 2022 of CMC University (established under Decision No. 46/2022/QĐ-ĐHCMC-NS dated December 12, 2022) held a meeting, reviewed, openly discussed, and voted to nominate for the appointment of the associate professor title for 05 dossiers.

The results of 05 dossiers met the required number of votes of confidence, and the Committee unanimously agreed to include them in the list of nominations, as follows:23 2022 TB-ĐHCMC-NS thông báo kết quả xét bổ nhiệm chức danh Phó giáo sư năm 2022
