## Announcement: Schedule for the Second Round Scholarship Awards in 2022

## The Scholarship Awarding Council of CMC University – institution code: CMC (formerly known as the Asia-Pacific Industrial Fine Arts University) hereby announces the schedule for the evaluation and awarding of scholarships for the second round in 2022:

### 1.Scholarships:

CMC Pioneer Scholarship:Full tuition fee coverage (equivalent to 198-234 million VND per scholarship depending on the specific field of study).

CMC Creative Scholarship:70% tuition fee coverage for the entire course.

CMC Vanguard Scholarship:50% tuition fee coverage for the entire course.

For detailed information regarding the 2022 Scholarship Policies and Financial Aid, applicants can find more details here:

### 2. Eligibility Criteria:

Applicants who have not registered for the CMC University Scholarship Review in the first round and have been admitted to the university through one of the following methods:

Admission based on high school academic performance (academic record) (CMC200).

Direct admission under CMC University’s admission scheme (CMC303).

Admission based on the results of the 2022 National Aptitude Assessment by Hanoi National University, Ho Chi Minh City National University, or the Cognitive Assessment Test by Hanoi University of Science and Technology (CMC402).

Applicants who have registered their intention to join CMC University through the admission method based on the results of the 2022 National High School Graduation Examination (CMC100).
