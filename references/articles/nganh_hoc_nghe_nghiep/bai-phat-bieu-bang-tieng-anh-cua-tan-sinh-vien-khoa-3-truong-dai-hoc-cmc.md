## Bài phát biểu bằng tiếng Anh của tân sinh viên Khóa 3 Trường Đại học CMC

Tại Lễ khai giảng Khóa 3 Trường Đại học CMC năm học 2024 – 2025: AI-X Pioneers, em Triệu Hồng Quân, sinh viên ngành Công nghệ Thông tin đại diện cho toàn thể tân sinh viên Khóa 3 phát biểu bằng tiếng Anh. Với phong thái tự tin và khả năng diễn đạt trôi chảy, Triệu Hồng Quân không chỉ truyền cảm hứng đến các bạn tân sinh viên mà còn để lại ấn tượng mạnh mẽ trong lòng thầy cô và các vị đại biểu tại buổi lễ.

Dưới đây là toàn văn bài phát biểu của em Triệu Hồng Quân tại Lễ khai giảng Khóa 3 năm học 2024 – 2025.

Good afternoon, respected faculty, staff, fellow students, and distinguished guests!

It’s an incredible honor to stand before you today as a representative of the incoming freshman class. My name is Quân, and like all of you, I am filled with excitement and anticipation for this new chapter in our lives.

As I look around, I see faces full of eagerness, curiosity, and maybe just a bit of nervousness. I feel the same way—as a recent high school graduate myself. We’ve all come from different places—some from nearby, others from far away—but today, we begin a shared journey, united by common goals and dreams.

Today marks the beginning of a new adventure. University life offers us a world of opportunities that extend far beyond the classroom. It’s not just about textbooks and lectures; it’s about discovering who we are, exploring new passions, and meeting people who will challenge and inspire us in ways we never imagined.

As students embarking on the journey of Digital and AI Transformation, we are entering fields that hold the power to reshape the world. Technology is transforming how we live and work, and AI offers endless potential. We have the chance to create smarter, more ethical solutions and make a lasting impact, not just in technology, but on the world.

Of course, with these new opportunities come inevitable challenges. We’ll encounter new responsibilities, academic pressures, and moments of uncertainty. It’s normal to feel a little nervous—we’re all in the same boat. But I believe that together, we can rise to the occasion. We can support one another, learn from each other, and create a community where we all thrive.

As we begin this new chapter, let us commit to embracing every opportunity that comes our way. Let’s embrace failure as a chance to grow. Seek knowledge not just for grades, but for wisdom. University life is about personal success, building relationships, contributing to communities, and helping others succeed.

In closing, I would like to say how excited I am to share this journey with all of you. Let’s make the most of every moment and create memories that will last a lifetime. Steve Jobs, the co-founder of Apple Inc., once famously said, “Stay hungry, stay foolish.” This spirit of curiosity and fearlessness is something we can all carry with us as we embark on this new chapter.

Thank you, and I wish everyone a successful and fulfilling academic year!
