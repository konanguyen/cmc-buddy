## CMC University accompanies Hai Phong students at the 2023 Enrollment and Career Counseling Day

On February 26th, the enrollment advisory team from CMC University participated in the “2023 Enrollment and Career Counseling Day” program organized by the Ministry of Education and Training, the Ministry of Labor, Invalids and Social Affairs, the Department of Education and Training of Hai Phong City, and the Hai Phong Youth Union in collaboration with Tuoi Tre newspaper. The program attracted the attention of nearly 7,000 high school students and parents in the area.

Starting at 7:00 AM on February 26th, the “2023 Enrollment and Career Counseling Day” took place at the Vietnam Maritime University in Hai Phong City. The event provided valuable information to address concerns and questions from high school students about the 2023 high school graduation exam, admission regulations from the Ministry of Education and Training, enrollment information, and various academic disciplines.

In the experiential space at booths 07-08 of CMC University, high school students and their parents listened to information about the admission process, enrollment quotas, training programs, facilities, scholarship policies, and tuition fee discounts for 2023. They also learned about student activities at CMC University.

In addition, candidates also received in-depth career advice on various fields of study and prospective job positions in the era of Industry 4.0. Particularly, they were informed about CMC University’s commitment to ensuring 100% employment at CMC Technology Corporation and Samsung for Information Technology graduates. With its prominent brand color of blue, the CMC University advisory area was vibrant and attracted a large number of interested candidates registering for consultations.

The event concluded after a long day filled with lively activities and meaningful experiences. Through the event, the 2005 candidates in Hai Phong received the most accurate and practical information to prepare for the upcoming exam, as well as guidance on how to prepare their applications, provided by the experienced admissions team of CMC University.
