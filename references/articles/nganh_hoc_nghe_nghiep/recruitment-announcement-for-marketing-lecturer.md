About CMC University

CMC University is a young and innovative institution, invested by CMC Corporation to develop as a pioneering technological and digital university in Vietnam, with the goal of becoming a globally prestigious university by 2043. The university’s mission is to create knowledge and train high-quality human resources in the fields of science, technology, and innovation, contributing to the building of a strong Vietnam.

With the philosophies of liberal and human-centered education, the university is a place for faculty, staff, and students to be creative, learn, research, work, develop personally, and serve society. The work environment is built and standardized according to international practices. In addition, faculty and students have opportunities to participate in applied research projects, technology development, and entrepreneurship within the ecosystem of enterprises, investment funds, research institutes, and universities of the CMC Corporation.

Recruitment Position: Marketing Lecturer

Job Description

Develop teaching plans, design, and update lecture content for one or more courses within the Marketing specialization.

Teach marketing courses including Introduction to Marketing, Social Media Marketing, Market Research, Global Brand Management, Product Development, Marketing Strategy and Planning, and other specialized marketing courses.

Conduct theoretical and practical teaching in class or online as per the school’s schedule and regulations.

Participate in professional development activities, curriculum development, supervise theses, oversee examinations, prepare exam questions, grade exams, etc.

Engage in scientific research as per the university’s regulations.

Perform other tasks as assigned by the Department Head and as directed by the Principal.

Requirements

Degree: Master’s/Ph.D. in Marketing/Business Administration, preferably from a foreign university.

Experience: Minimum of 2 years of teaching experience in Marketing courses at an accredited university.

Research experience in the field, with a preference for international publications.

Ability to read and research materials in English.

Good pedagogical and communication skills.

Passionate, dedicated, and enthusiastic about teaching, especially knowledgeable about active learning, problem-based learning, and case teaching methods.

Enthusiastic and committed to working with students and colleagues.

Competitive salary, negotiable based on skills, attractive annual income package as per CMC Corporation’s policy.

Healthy, dynamic, friendly working environment with clear career progression.

Flexible working hours based on the teaching schedule.

Access to training courses offered by the University and the Corporation.

Support for training and certification to enhance pedagogical and professional skills.

Benefits for employees as per current legal regulations (social insurance, health insurance, unemployment insurance, etc.).

Special health care package for staff and lecturers of the University.

Bonus during holidays, New Year, Lunar New Year; annual KPIs bonus.

Participation in extracurricular activities: cultural, sports, travel organized by the school and the Corporation
